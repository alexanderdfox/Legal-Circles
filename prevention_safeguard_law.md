# Draft Bill: The Constitutional Architecture and Institutional Integrity Act

**An Act** to establish procedural safeguards against the arbitrary expansion of the federal judiciary, to standardize requirements for the admission of new states, and to preserve the constitutional balance of powers through statutory enforcement.

---

## Section 1: Title
This Act may be cited as the "Constitutional Architecture and Institutional Integrity Act."

## Section 2: Stabilization of the Supreme Court Membership
1. **Fixed Composition:** The Supreme Court of the United States shall permanently consist of nine (9) justices: one (1) Chief Justice of the United States and eight (8) Associate Justices.
2. **Procedural Firewall (Supermajority Requirement):** Any subsequent legislative proposal, bill, or resolution that seeks to amend Title 28, United States Code, Section 1, to alter the number of seated justices on the Supreme Court shall require an affirmative vote of three-fifths (60%) of the members duly chosen and sworn in both the House of Representatives and the Senate.

## Section 3: Objective Standards for Statehood Admission
No territory, district, or possession may be admitted into the Union as a new State under Article IV of the Constitution unless it meets the following minimum criteria:
1. **Population Threshold:** The territory must possess a resident population equal to or greater than the population of the least populous existing State, as verified by the most recent decennial census.
2. **Geographic Integrity:** The territory must possess a distinct geographic boundary that does not overlap with or partition an existing federal district or State capital, unless explicitly consented to by a three-fourths (75%) supermajority vote of Congress.
3. **Plebiscite:** A minimum of sixty percent (60%) of registered voters within the territory must vote in favor of statehood via a certified referendum.

## Section 4: Jurisdiction Stripping and Safeguards
1. **Fast-Track Constitutional Challenge:** Any Act of Congress that purports to alter the size of the Supreme Court or admit a new State in violation of Section 2 or Section 3 of this Act shall be subject to immediate, accelerated review by a special three-judge panel of the United States Court of Appeals for the District of Columbia Circuit.
2. **Injunction Power:** The reviewing court shall possess the authority to issue a mandatory nationwide injunction staying the seating of any new judges or congressional representatives until a full constitutional determination is rendered.

## Section 5: Severability and Supremacy
If any provision of this Act, or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance, is held to be unconstitutional, the remainder of this Act shall not be affected thereby, and shall remain in full force and effect to protect the core framework of the Republic.
